# Analysis protocols

These dated records describe the analysis populations, comparisons, weights,
endpoints, uncertainty calculations and stopping rules used in the study.
The dates refer to the original analysis plans. They do not imply public
preregistration; the manuscript distinguishes prespecified and post hoc work.

The public copies were edited on 16 October 2026 to remove conversational
project-management notes, internal scoring targets and speculative planning
probabilities. Analysis definitions, numerical thresholds and amendment history
are retained. The original files are preserved in the study archive; the
provenance record distinguishes the original and public-copy hashes.

Historical execution summaries should be read alongside subsequent corrections.
In particular, the July PACC clinical-severity claim was withdrawn after the
October residual-scale correction. The corrected estimate is 0.0184151 with a
95% interval of [-0.0025473, 0.0381420]. The manuscript and supplementary results
give the current interpretation.
